A little cabinet for the unusual, the obscure, the beautiful, the forgotten and the things I happen to stumble upon. Some are discoveries, some are curiosities, and some are simply things I don't want to lose.

DRAWER NO. I

Strange Facts

Small facts about the world that seem almost invented.

  • Cleopatra lived closer in time to the first moon landing than to the construction of the Great Pyramid of Giza.
  • Oxford University existed before the Aztec Empire.
  • A day on Venus is longer than its year.
  • The Eiffel Tower can become slightly taller in hot weather because metal expands.

DRAWER NO. III

Unusual Objects

Objects, inventions and things with peculiar histories.

  • Automata: mechanical figures designed to imitate movement, often created centuries ago.
  • Curiosity cabinets: collections of unusual objects once kept by collectors as miniature museums.
  • Hourglasses: ancient timekeeping devices that measure time through flowing sand.
  • Palimpsests: manuscripts where older writing was erased or overwritten, leaving traces behind.
DRAWER NO. IV

Unsolved Mysteries

Historical mysteries, strange records and unanswered questions.

  • The Voynich Manuscript: a historical manuscript written in a script that remains undeciphered.
  • The Antikythera Mechanism: an ancient Greek device whose complexity continues to fascinate researchers.
  • The Nazca Lines: enormous geoglyphs created in the desert of southern Peru.
  • Historical lost languages: languages whose writing systems or meanings are still only partly understood.

DRAWER NO. VI

Forgotten & Obscure

Forgotten words, lesser-known stories and things from the margins.

  • Apricity: the warmth of the sun on a cold winter day.
  • Epistolary: a literary form involving letters or correspondence.
  • Wunderkammer: German for a cabinet of wonders, a collection of extraordinary objects.
  • Palimpsest: something that retains traces of an earlier form beneath a newer one.
