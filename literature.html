Writers whose works, ideas and worlds have found a place in my personal library.

Donna Tartt

American novelist · Contemporary literature

Donna Tartt is an American novelist known for her carefully constructed narratives, atmospheric settings and explorations of friendship, obsession, guilt and belonging.

Notable works

The Secret History · The Little Friend · The Goldfinch

Fyodor Dostoevsky

Russian novelist · 19th century

Dostoevsky explored the psychological and moral struggles of individuals. His novels examine faith, freedom, suffering, guilt and the contradictions of human nature.

Notable works

Crime and Punishment · The Brothers Karamazov · The Idiot · Notes from Underground

Friedrich Nietzsche

German philosopher · 19th century

Nietzsche was a philosopher whose work questioned traditional morality, religion and cultural values. His writing deals with individuality, creativity, power and the meaning of existence.

Notable works

Thus Spoke Zarathustra · Beyond Good and Evil · The Birth of Tragedy · On the Genealogy of Morality

Franz Kafka

German-language writer · Modernist literature

Kafka is known for unsettling stories about alienation, bureaucracy, uncertainty and the individual's relationship with systems that seem impossible to understand.

Notable works

The Metamorphosis · The Trial · The Castle · The Judgment

Albert Camus

French-Algerian writer · Philosopher

Camus examined the absurdity of human existence and the search for meaning in an indifferent world. His works often explore freedom, responsibility and moral choices.

Notable works

The Stranger · The Plague · The Myth of Sisyphus · The Fall

Clarice Lispector

Brazilian writer · Modernist literature

Lispector is recognised for her introspective and experimental writing. Her stories explore consciousness, identity, ordinary experiences and the complexity of inner life.

Notable works

The Hour of the Star · Near to the Wild Heart · The Passion According to G.H.

Ocean Vuong

Vietnamese-American writer · Contemporary literature

Vuong is a poet and novelist whose work often considers memory, family, language, migration and the ways personal histories shape identity.

Notable works

On Earth We're Briefly Gorgeous · Night Sky with Exit Wounds · Time Is a Mother

Hanya Yanagihara

American novelist · Contemporary literature

Yanagihara writes novels about friendship, memory, ambition and the complicated relationships people form with one another.

Notable works

A Little Life · The People in the Trees · To Paradise

Susan Sontag

American writer · Critic and essayist

Sontag wrote influential essays on art, photography, literature and culture. Her work questions how people interpret images, aesthetics and artistic expression.

Notable works

On Photography · Against Interpretation · Regarding the Pain of Others

Hermann Hesse

German-Swiss writer · 20th century

Hesse's novels frequently explore self-discovery, spirituality, individuality and the tension between personal desires and social expectations.

Notable works

Steppenwolf · Siddhartha · Demian · The Glass Bead Game

Toni Morrison

American novelist · Nobel Prize in Literature

Morrison's writing examines memory, identity, history, community and the experiences of Black Americans. Her novels are celebrated for their language and narrative depth.

Notable works

Beloved · The Bluest Eye · Song of Solomon · Sula

Oscar Wilde

Irish writer · Aesthetic movement

Wilde was known for his wit, social criticism and distinctive literary style. His works often explore beauty, identity, hypocrisy and the conventions of society.

Notable works

The Picture of Dorian Gray · The Importance of Being Earnest · De Profundis

Simone de Beauvoir

French writer · Philosopher

De Beauvoir was a philosopher, novelist and essayist whose work examined freedom, ethics, social expectations and the construction of identity.

Notable works

The Second Sex · She Came to Stay · The Ethics of Ambiguity · Memoirs of a Dutiful Daughter

Sylvia Plath

American poet and novelist · 20th century

Plath is remembered for her precise, intense poetry and distinctive imagery. Her writing explores identity, relationships, creativity and the pressures of everyday life.

Notable works

The Bell Jar · Ariel · The Colossus and Other Poems
