Welcome, stranger.

A small collection of things I find beautiful, thoughts I don't want to lose, books I've read, music I return to, and fragments of everyday life. Consider this a little corner of the internet I made entirely my own.

Feel free to wander. There is no particular order to things here.

A little introduction

I'm Umida, a student of English Language Teaching with an interest in literature, art, music, and the many ways people express and interpret the world around them.

Beyond my academic studies, I enjoy photography, cinema, writing, and exploring subjects ranging from linguistics and psychology to philosophy and history.

This website serves as a personal collection of these interests, bringing together things I enjoy, ideas I explore, and materials I wish to preserve.

It is less of a finished project and more of a growing archive. Some things may change, some may remain unfinished, and others may appear unexpectedly.
